01 — About

Background

I am a Principal Computer Engineer with over two decades of combined academic and industrial experience in artificial intelligence algorithm development and embedded systems design.

Currently serving as Director of Engineering at Kudan, I lead the development of cutting-edge SLAM (Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping) systems for industrial applications. My expertise spans computer vision, signal processing, and machine learning, with deep proficiency in C++/OpenCV and MATLAB.

Prior to my industry career, I spent nearly a decade as an academic lecturer, teaching topics ranging from fundamental principles to advanced concepts in algorithm design and AI programming.
